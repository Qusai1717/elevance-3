Terms of Service

Last updated: July 2026

By booking a service with ElevanceYVR, you agree to the following terms.

1. Services

We provide mobile car detailing at your location within Metro Vancouver. Package descriptions on this website outline what is included. Services not listed (such as engine bay cleaning or headlight restoration) are not offered at this time.

2. Pricing & Payment

  • Advertised prices are starting prices for sedans in average condition. Larger vehicles and heavily soiled interiors may increase the price; we always confirm the final price with you before starting work.
  • Payment is due on completion, by credit card (processed by Square), debit card, cash, or Interac e-transfer.

3. Bookings, Cancellations & Weather

  • Booking requests are confirmed by phone, text, or email before they are final.
  • Please provide at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • If weather prevents quality work, we will reschedule your appointment at no charge.

4. Site Requirements

We bring our own water and power. You are responsible for providing legal, accessible parking with reasonable space to work around the vehicle, and for confirming your building permits mobile detailing where applicable.

5. Vehicle Condition & Personal Items

  • Please remove valuables and personal items before your appointment. We are not responsible for items left in the vehicle.
  • Pre-existing damage (scratches, tears, stains that cannot be fully removed, worn surfaces) may be documented before work begins and is not our responsibility.

6. Satisfaction & Liability

We perform a final walkaround with you on completion. If something isn't right, tell us before we leave and we'll make it right. Our liability is limited to the amount paid for the service.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms? Contact us at qusaisherif@elevanceyvr.com or 825-733-4385.
