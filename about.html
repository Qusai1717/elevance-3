About Us

Obsessed With the Details

ElevanceYVR was built on a simple idea: your car deserves showroom care, and you shouldn't have to leave home to get it.

Our Story

We started ElevanceYVR because we love cars — and because we noticed most "detailing" in the Lower Mainland was really just a fast wash with a fancy name. We wanted to bring true, meticulous detailing to Metro Vancouver, and deliver it in the most convenient way possible: right to your driveway.

Today we serve homes, apartments, and workplaces from Langley to West Vancouver, with a perfect 5-star Google rating and customers who book us again and again. Every vehicle gets the same treatment — whether it's a daily commuter or a weekend exotic.
