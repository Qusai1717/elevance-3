FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your mobile detail.

Most appointments take between 2 and 5 hours depending on the service and your vehicle's size and condition. Exterior Detailing typically runs 1.5–2 hours, Interior Detailing 2–3 hours, and a Full Detail 4–5 hours. We confirm a time estimate when we confirm your booking.

We serve all of Metro Vancouver — Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Surrey, Coquitlam, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, New Westminster, Delta, and Langley — at your home, apartment, or workplace.

We accept credit cards (processed securely through Square), debit cards, cash, and Interac e-transfer. Payment is collected once your detail is complete and you've inspected the results.

Listed prices reflect sedans in average condition. Larger vehicles and heavily soiled interiors require more time and product, so your final quote may vary. We always confirm your exact price before any work begins — no surprises.
