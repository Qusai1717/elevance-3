How Often Should You Detail Your Car in Vancouver?

Published July 6, 2026 · by the ElevanceYVR team

Vancouver is one of the toughest cities in Canada on vehicle finishes. Between eight months of rain, winter road salt on the bridges, spring pollen, and summer tree sap, your car's paint and interior take a beating year-round. Here's the detailing schedule we recommend for Lower Mainland drivers.

Exterior: Every 3–4 Months

Rain isn't the rinse it looks like — it carries contaminants that sit on your clear coat, and our region's road film builds up fast. A professional exterior detail every season removes bonded grime and refreshes your wax protection before damage sets in. If your car lives outside (no garage or carport), lean toward every 3 months.

Interior: Every 4–6 Months

Wet shoes, coffee commutes, dogs, kids, beach days — Vancouver interiors collect moisture and grime that lead to odours and premature wear. A deep interior detail twice a year keeps carpets, seats, and trim in like-new condition. If you drive daily with passengers or pets, every 4 months makes a visible difference.

Full Detail: Twice a Year

We recommend a Full Detail at two key moments: late fall (protection before the wet season and road salt) and late spring (undo winter's damage, protect against summer UV and sap). Many of our regulars book these in advance and fill the gaps with quicker maintenance details.

Signs You're Overdue

Water no longer beads on the paint. The interior has a smell that airing out doesn't fix. Wheels have brown deposits that don't rinse off. Windows haze up quickly. If any of these sound familiar, your car is ready for professional attention.
